User Data Deletion

Instructions for requesting deletion of customer information

To request deletion of information associated with your customer communication, email keshariya2012@gmail.com.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to keshariya2012@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  3. Provide enough information for us to identify the relevant customer record, without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

What happens next

We will review the request and, where applicable and legally permissible, delete or anonymize the requested information from systems under our control. Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate business records, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or other lawful purposes.

WhatsApp/Meta data

Information processed directly by WhatsApp or Meta may be subject to their own data controls and policies. Requests concerning data held by those services may need to be submitted to them directly.
