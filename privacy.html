Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Keshariya handles information processed through WhatsApp Business Desktop and related WhatsApp Business Platform integrations.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the business communication being performed, the application may process customer contact details such as phone numbers and names, message content, message status information, template parameters, and media or documents that are intentionally sent or received through the service.

2. How information is used

  • To communicate with customers about purchases, services, offers, support, and other business interactions.
  • To send approved WhatsApp message templates through the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • To maintain message delivery, delivery-status, and operational records.
  • To troubleshoot, secure, and maintain the application.

3. WhatsApp and Meta

WhatsApp messaging is provided through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Information transmitted through that platform is also subject to the applicable WhatsApp and Meta terms and privacy practices.

4. Data sharing

Information is not sold for advertising purposes by Keshariya. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application and by Meta/WhatsApp when using the WhatsApp Business Platform.

5. Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect business and customer information. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for business records, customer communication, operational troubleshooting, legal obligations, or other legitimate business purposes. Information that is no longer required may be deleted.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact Keshariya to ask about information associated with your customer communication or to request deletion where applicable.
